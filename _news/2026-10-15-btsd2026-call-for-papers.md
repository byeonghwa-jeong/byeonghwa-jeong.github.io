I am serving on the **Program Committee** for the **6th International Workshop on Big Data & AI-Assisted Tools, Methods, and Use Cases for Innovative Scientific Discovery (BTSD 2026)**, held in conjunction with **IEEE BigData 2026**.

The workshop brings together research on AI-assisted scientific discovery, research copilots, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), scientific workflows, HPC/cloud computing, and real-world scientific applications.

The paper submission deadline has been extended to **October 29, 2026**.
