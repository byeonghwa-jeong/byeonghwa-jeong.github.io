I am serving on the **Program Committee** for the **6th International Workshop on Big Data & AI-Assisted Tools, Methods, and Use Cases for Innovative Scientific Discovery (BTSD 2026)**, held in conjunction with **IEEE BigData 2026**.

The workshop brings together research on AI-assisted scientific discovery, research copilots, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), scientific workflows, HPC and cloud computing, and real-world scientific applications.

The paper submission deadline has been extended to **October 29, 2026**.

**Workshop:** BTSD 2026 - 6th International Workshop on Big Data & AI-Assisted Tools, Methods, and Use Cases for Innovative Scientific Discovery

**Conference:** IEEE BigData 2026

**Location:** Phoenix, Arizona

**Conference dates:** December 14-17, 2026

**Extended submission deadline:** October 29, 2026

Researchers working on AI-assisted scientific discovery, scientific workflows, RAG, research copilots, HPC and cloud computing, and related applications are encouraged to submit their work.
